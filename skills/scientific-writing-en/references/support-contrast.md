#### Writing practice

1. Using phrases from the section above, explain the main points of your text
2. Using the words in italics in the section above, write five new phrases for your text
3. In class, in groups or in pairs, exchange texts and evaluate each other’s writing, going through the points above

## Additional phrases

### Contrasting

- This interpretation has recently been put to the test; however...
- Although the initial results suggest X, subsequent studies have shown that...
- Although there are numerous studies on...
- Considerable disagreement remains regarding X...
- X has been the subject of heated debate in the literature...
- The point of contention was...
- A source / focus of controversy has been...
- Controversy continues to surround...
- Author and Author's response to X is highly critical of...
- Author's views have failed to gain widespread acceptance in the scientific community...
- X was criticized for failing to take into account...
- There are growing doubts regarding...
- Many scholars have doubts about...
- Few authors are willing to discuss...
- An alternative to X proposed by Author is...
- X is beginning to gain acceptance; however, it is not without its critics...
- Unlike past research... this study...
- There appears to be no compelling reason to maintain...
- In response to this point, Author argues...
- The underlying counter-argument is...

- A similar view is held by Author...
- See, for example, Author's articles in...

- As Author also notes...
- As Author also emphasizes...
- Author's study likely comes closest to our viewpoint...
- There are X arguments in support of...
- The underlying argument in favor is...
