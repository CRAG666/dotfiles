#### Writing practice

1. Look at the vocabulary in your text
   - a. Using the headings in the thesaurus, think how you can improve any expressions in your text
   - b. In what way do the words differ (use a dictionary if necessary)?
2. In class, in groups or in pairs, exchange texts and evaluate each other’s writing, going through the points above
